Privacy note

This is a simple information website for Psusennes Developments. We keep it light on data.

What we collect

This site has one form, the brochure form. It has no accounts and no tracking or advertising cookies of our own.

The brochure form

What we collect. When you ask for the villa brochure, we collect your name, your email address, your WhatsApp number if you give it, your country, whether you are interested in buying, renting or both, and the language and page of the form. Our server also records the date and time, your browser type (user agent) and your IP address. The form has two hidden fields that help us block spam; they do not identify you.

Why. We use these details only to send you information about the villas and to answer your questions, and only because you tick the consent box. The date, browser type and IP address help us spot spam and abuse.

Where it goes. Your details are stored in a file on our web hosting (Hostinger), outside the public part of the website, and are emailed to our own team mailbox so we can reply to you.

We never sell your data. We never sell your details, and we do not share them with other companies for their marketing.

How long. We keep them while we are in touch with you about the villas, and delete them when you ask.

Currency

To show prices in your currency, the site stores your choice in a cookie for one year, may look up your country from your IP address (ipapi.co) and loads exchange rates from open.er-api.com. The official price is always in Indonesian Rupiah.

WhatsApp, email and social links

When you tap a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens with a suggested message that you can edit before sending. Anything you send us there, or by email to info@psues.com, is used only to reply to you about our villas and services. WhatsApp, Instagram and YouTube have their own privacy policies.

Third-party services

Images and videos load from Cloudinary and fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any web server, these services and our host may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the files.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your messages or your brochure form details deleted, contact us on WhatsApp at +62 812-2863-6230 or by email at info@psues.com. We will confirm when your details are deleted.
