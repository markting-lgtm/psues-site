Who we are

We build it. We market it.

Psusennes Developments is a real estate development and marketing company in Bali. We develop our own villas from scratch, starting with Psusennes Villas in Munggu, and we use the same skills to market, sell and manage property for other owners. Every project gets the full treatment: design, photography, video and social media, and a direct WhatsApp line to the people doing the work.
