Psusennes Villas are three villas built to one design in Munggu, in the Seseh area of south-west Bali, between Canggu, Pererenan and Seseh, about 10 minutes from Seseh Beach. The villas are under construction and the build is fully funded to completion, planned for February 2027.

Each villa has three bedrooms, each with its own ensuite. The master bedroom has a private balcony and a larger ensuite with a polished terrazzo bathtub. Bedroom 2 is a bunk room. There is a powder room on the ground floor and one open-plan space for living, dining and the kitchen. Outside there is a private pool with a bangkirai sun deck, and the rooftop has a lounge and a dining area with a sink and BBQ. A carport takes one car.
