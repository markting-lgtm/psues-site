Long-term villa rental in Bali: a home in Munggu, near Canggu and Seseh

Looking for a long term villa rental in Bali, close to Canggu but away from its busiest streets? Munggu, in the Seseh area of south-west Bali, sits between Canggu, Pererenan and Seseh, about 10 minutes from Seseh Beach. This guide explains what living in Munggu is like, what you would be renting at Psusennes Villas, and what to ask before you sign any long-term rental.

Why look beyond central Canggu

Many people searching for a Canggu long term rental want two things at once: easy access to the cafes, shops and coworking spaces of Canggu, and a quieter place to come home to. Munggu offers that balance. Canggu and Pererenan are a short drive away, while the village itself keeps a calm, residential feel, with rice fields still opening up between the houses.

Seseh Beach is about 10 minutes from the villas: close enough for a morning walk by the sea or the evening light over the water, and far enough that the streets around you stay quiet. Our Seseh Beach and Munggu area guide describes the area in more detail.

Who a long-term villa in Munggu suits

  • Families who want space, a private pool and a quieter street.
  • Remote workers who want calm working days with Canggu within reach.
  • Long-stay residents who would rather live in a village than in a busy centre.
  • Friends or couples who want a bedroom and bathroom each, with room for visiting guests.

It suits people less if they want nightlife at the door every evening. For that, central Canggu is closer to what you are looking for.

The villa: what you would be renting

Psusennes Villas are three villas of one design in Munggu. Each villa has:

  • Three ensuite bedrooms, plus a powder room for guests.
  • A master bedroom with its own balcony and a polished terrazzo bathtub.
  • Bedroom 2 set up as a bunk room, which works well for children or extra guests.
  • A private pool with a bangkirai sun deck.
  • A rooftop lounge with a dining area and BBQ.
  • A carport.

For a long stay, the layout matters more than it does for a holiday. A bedroom and bathroom for each person, a room ready for guests and an outdoor space on the roof make it easier to live and work in the same house for months at a time.

What to ask before you sign a long-term rental

Whichever villa you rent in Bali, ask these questions and get the answers in writing:

  • The rent and how it is paid: the amount, the currency, the payment schedule and any deposit.
  • What is included: electricity, water, internet, pool and garden care and cleaning, and who pays for each.
  • Repairs: who to contact when something breaks, and how quickly it is handled.
  • The term: the start and end dates, how to extend, and the notice period on both sides.
  • House rules: guests, pets, and anything the neighbourhood expects.
  • Your visa: check that your stay permit covers the whole length of your stay.
  • A written agreement: signed by both sides, with an inventory of what is in the villa.

Getting around from Munggu

Most people in this part of Bali get around by car or scooter, or use ride apps or a local driver. Travel times depend a lot on traffic and the time of day, so try the routes you will use most, to Canggu, Pererenan or your coworking space, before you commit to a long stay.
