Seseh Beach and Munggu: Area Guide

Seseh Beach and the village of Munggu sit on the south-west coast of Bali, just beyond Canggu and Pererenan. If you have spent time in Canggu, this is the quieter stretch a little further along the coast: more rice fields, fewer crowds, and a slower pace of life. This guide explains where Munggu and Seseh are, what the area is like, and what it is like to live in a Seseh villa here.

Where are Munggu and Seseh?

Munggu is a village in south-west Bali, inland from the coast. Seseh is the neighbouring coastal area, and Seseh Beach is its stretch of shoreline. Together they lie between Canggu, Pererenan and Seseh, so the busier parts of Canggu are close by, while the area itself keeps a calmer, more residential feel.

From our villas in Munggu, Seseh Beach is about 10 minutes away. That short distance is what shapes daily life here: you can spend the morning by the sea and be back at your own pool before lunch.

What the area is like

The first thing most visitors notice is the space. Rice fields still open up between the houses and villas, and the roads are quieter than in the centre of Canggu. Mornings start early, with farmers in the fields and the sound of the village around you. Evenings are calm.

It is still very much a Balinese village. Daily life follows the local community and its ceremonies, and you will see offerings at doorways and small shrines along the way. Living here well means being a considerate neighbour: drive slowly through the village, dress respectfully near temples, and give way to ceremonies on the road.

For people who want to be near the energy of Canggu without living in the middle of it, this balance is the main attraction. You can head out when you want company, and come home to your own space when you do not.

Seseh Beach

Seseh Beach is part of the long west-facing coastline that runs north from Canggu. Like much of this coast, it is a natural beach rather than a developed resort strip, and it tends to feel more open and less busy than the best-known beaches nearby. It is a good place for a walk along the water, for watching the sea, and for the evening light over the ocean.

The sea on this coast can be strong. If you plan to swim, check the local conditions on the day, swim only where it is safe and follow any flags or local advice.

Getting around

Most people in this part of Bali get around by car or scooter, and many residents use ride apps or a local driver. Canggu and Pererenan are a short drive away for shopping, cafes and restaurants. Travel times in Bali depend a lot on traffic and the time of day, so it is worth trying the routes you will use most before you decide where to live.

Who the area suits

  • Families who want space, a private pool and a quieter street, with the beach close by.
  • Remote workers and long-stay residents who want calm days and easy access to Canggu when they need it.
  • Owners who want to rent their villa out to guests looking for a quieter base near the coast.

It suits people less if they want to walk out of the door straight into nightlife every evening. For that, central Canggu is closer to what you are looking for.
