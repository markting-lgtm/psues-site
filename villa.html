The villa

Every room has its own story

Psusennes Villas are three villas built to one design in Munggu, in the Seseh area of Bali, about 10 minutes from Seseh Beach. The villas are under construction and the build is fully funded to completion, planned for February 2027.

Each villa has three bedrooms, each with its own ensuite. The master bedroom has a private balcony and a larger ensuite with a polished terrazzo bathtub. There is a powder room on the ground floor and one open-plan space for living, dining and the kitchen.

Outside you have a private pool, a terrace and sunbeds. Upstairs, the rooftop has a lounge and a dining area with a sink and BBQ, with rice-field and ocean views. A carport takes one car.

Leasehold 30 years + renewal. Available to buy or to rent.
