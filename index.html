Bali villa for sale in Munggu, near Seseh Beach

Psusennes Villas are three new villas of one design for sale in Munggu, in the Seseh area of south-west Bali, between Canggu, Pererenan and Seseh Beach. The beach is about 10 minutes away. Each villa has three bedrooms, each with its own ensuite, a powder room for guests, a private pool, a rooftop lounge with BBQ and a carport.

Questions buyers ask

How much is a villa?

IDR 5,700,000,000 per villa at the off-plan price. After completion the price is IDR 6,200,000,000. Prices are in Indonesian Rupiah; this site can also show them in your currency.

When will the villas be finished?

Completion is planned for February 2027. The villas are under construction and you can follow the work on our Progress page.

What does leasehold 30 years + renewal mean?

You hold the right to the villa for 30 years under a notarised lease, with a renewal option. This is the usual way foreign buyers hold villas in Bali.

Can I see the villa before I buy?

Walk through the design online on our Villa Tour and Floor Plan pages, then message us on WhatsApp to visit the site in Munggu.

Can you manage the villa as a rental for me?

Yes. Villa rental management is one of our services. Ask us on WhatsApp for details.
